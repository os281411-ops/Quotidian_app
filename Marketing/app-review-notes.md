Thank you for reviewing Quotidian 2.0.

WHAT'S NEW
Quotidian shows one quote from a great book each day. Version 2.0 adds Bookclub (reading groups with chat), a shared daily discussion of the day's quote, and a Bookshelf where readers add books by scanning a barcode or searching.

SIGNING IN
The daily quote, Library, Bookshelf and streaks need no account. Only Bookclub needs one, and it uses Sign in with Apple only, so no demo username or password is needed: please sign in with any Apple ID in the Bookclub tab, choose a username and accept the house rules. A private chat called "Welcome to Bookclub" in My Clubs gives a quick tour (type "next" to move through it).

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
- Terms: before posting, every user must accept house rules stating there is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.
- Filtering: messages, club names, usernames and spoiler labels are checked against a list of blocked words on our server before they're saved, and posting is rate-limited.
- Reporting: press and hold any message to report it, or report a person from their profile. A message reported by three different people is hidden automatically.
- Blocking: block from a message or a profile. Blocked users' messages disappear for the blocker straight away, in every club. Blocks can be undone in Bookclub → Account → Blocked Readers.
- Acting on reports: the developer reviews every report within 24 hours using an in-app moderation screen, and can remove messages and ban accounts. Banned accounts can no longer post or join clubs.
- Contact: quotidian.support@gmail.com (also shown in the app under Profile → Help & Feedback, and in the house rules).

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Bookclub → person icon → Delete Account. The user confirms with Sign in with Apple; our server then revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access through Apple's REST API and permanently deletes the account and everything posted.

SUBSCRIPTION
Quotidian Premium (auto-renewable, monthly or yearly) unlocks starting book clubs, joining private clubs by invite, unlimited Library saves, the home screen widget, styled share cards, colour themes, a monthly streak freeze and a second reminder. To try private clubs, you can join one with invite code [INVITE CODE] (Bookclub → + → Join with Invite Code) after subscribing in the sandbox.

CAMERA
The camera is used only to scan the barcode on a book (Bookshelf → + → Scan a Barcode). Only the ISBN number is sent to Open Library to look the book up; no images are stored or sent. Search works without the camera.

BOOKSHOP.ORG LINKS
Book links open Bookshop.org (physical books from independent bookshops) in the browser. We earn a standard affiliate commission; no digital content is sold outside the App Store.

Thank you!
