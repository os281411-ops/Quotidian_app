## Description (4000 char max)
Quotidian brings you one carefully chosen quote a day, from sixty of the world's great novels, memoirs and biographies (Moby-Dick to Beloved, Frankenstein to Becoming) and from the people who've said things worth remembering.

Read it, save it, and take a moment to reflect: how did it make you feel? Then talk it through with other readers in Bookclub, and keep every book you read on your Bookshelf.

FEATURES

• Daily Quote — a new quote every day, the same for everyone, so there's always something to talk about
• Discuss Today's Quote — one shared conversation a day about the day's quote, open to every reader
• Reflect — note how a quote made you feel and add your own thoughts, saved privately on your phone
• Library — keep the quotes that stop you in your tracks, with your reflections alongside
• Bookclub — join reading groups and chat: share passages, attach books, see what the club is reading now, and hide spoilers until everyone catches up
• Bookshelf — scan a book's barcode or search for it to add it to your shelf. Tap a cover to flip it over, rate it out of five stars and write down what you thought
• Streaks — keep a daily reading habit alive, with milestone celebrations at 7, 30, 100 and 365 days
• Reminders — an optional daily nudge so you never miss today's quote
• Shop the Book — books link straight to Bookshop.org, supporting independent bookshops with every purchase

QUOTIDIAN PREMIUM

Start your own book clubs, join private clubs with an invite, save without limits, and unlock the home screen widget, shareable quote cards, a monthly streak freeze, colour themes and a second daily reminder.

PRIVACY

The daily quote, your Library, Bookshelf, streaks and reflections need no account and stay on your phone. Bookclub is optional: it uses Sign in with Apple and stores your username and what you post so your clubs can see it. No ads, no tracking.

Quotidian 2.0 is our biggest update yet.

## Age Rating
No explicit sexual content, drug use or graphic violence is depicted. Quotes are short literary excerpts; some source books deal with serious historical subjects (the Holocaust, slavery, war), referenced only in brief, non-graphic quotations.
**Bookclub adds user-generated content and chat between users.** In the age rating questionnaire, answer **Yes** to user-generated content / messaging between users. With moderation, reporting and blocking in place, expect around 13+; the privacy policy also says Bookclub isn't for under-13s. It's a self-certification and the final call is yours.

## App Privacy (Data Collection)
In App Store Connect → App Privacy, declare (all **linked to the user**, purpose **App Functionality**, **not used for tracking**):
- **Contact Info → Name**: display name, if Apple shares it at first sign-in
- **Identifiers → User ID**: the Bookclub account ID
- **User Content → Other User Content**: messages, quoted passages, attached books, reports

Not collected: email address (never requested from Apple), photos or camera images (barcode scanning happens on the phone; nothing is saved or sent except the ISBN number, to Open Library, to look the book up), location, contacts, browsing history, purchases (Apple handles them), diagnostics, usage data.
Reflections, Library, Bookshelf (books, ratings, thoughts) and streaks stay on the device and don't count as collected.
