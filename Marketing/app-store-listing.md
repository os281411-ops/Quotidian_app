## Description (4000 char max)
Quotidian brings you one carefully chosen quote a day, from sixty of the world's great novels, memoirs and biographies (Moby-Dick to Beloved, Frankenstein to Becoming) and from the people who've said things worth remembering.

Read it, save it, and take a moment to reflect: how did it make you feel? Then talk it through with other readers in Bookclub.

FEATURES

• Daily Quote — a new quote every day, the same for everyone, so there's always something to talk about
• Reflect — note how a quote made you feel and add your own thoughts, saved privately on your phone
• Library — keep the quotes that stop you in your tracks, with your reflections alongside
• Bookclub — join reading groups and chat: share passages, attach books, tag what the club is reading, and hide spoilers until people catch up
• Streaks — keep a daily reading habit alive, with milestone celebrations at 7, 30, 100 and 365 days
• Reminders — an optional daily nudge so you never miss today's quote
• Shop the Book — quotes from books link straight to them on Bookshop.org, supporting independent bookstores with every purchase

QUOTIDIAN PREMIUM

Start your own book clubs, join private clubs with an invite, save without limits, and unlock the home screen widget, shareable quote cards, a monthly streak freeze, colour themes and a second daily reminder.

PRIVACY

The daily quote, your Library, streaks and reflections need no account and stay on your phone. Bookclub is optional: it uses Sign in with Apple and stores your username and what you post so your clubs can see it. No ads, no tracking.

## Age Rating
No explicit sexual content, drug use, or graphic violence is depicted — quotes are short literary excerpts. Some source books deal with serious historical subjects (the Holocaust, slavery, war), referenced only in brief, non-graphic quotations.
**Bookclub adds user-generated content and chat between users.** Apple's age rating questionnaire asks about this specifically (user-generated content / messaging), and it pushes the rating up. With moderation, reporting and blocking in place, expect around 13+; the privacy policy also says Bookclub isn't for under-13s. It's a self-certification and the final call is yours in App Store Connect.

## App Privacy (Data Collection)
With Bookclub, the answer is no longer "Data Not Collected". In App Store Connect → App Privacy, declare (all **linked to the user**, purpose **App Functionality**, **not used for tracking**):
- **Contact Info → Name**: display name, if Apple shares it at first sign-in
- **Identifiers → User ID**: the Bookclub account ID
- **User Content → Other User Content**: messages, quoted passages, attached books, reports
- **User Content → Customer Support**: only if you count support emails (they arrive by email, not through the app, so you can leave this off)
Not collected: email address (never requested from Apple), location, contacts, browsing history, purchases (Apple handles them), diagnostics, usage data.
Reflections, Library and streaks stay on the device and don't count as collected.
Privacy Policy URL: https://os281411-ops.github.io/Quotidian_app/privacy-policy.html
