Privacy Policy

Quotidian for iOS — Last updated October 27, 2026

Quotidian is a daily-quote app with an optional book club feature. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

Short version

The daily quote, your Library, Bookshelf, streaks, reflections and reminders need no account, and that information stays on your phone. If you choose to use Bookclub, you sign in with Apple and we store what's needed to run it: your username, the clubs you're in, and the messages you post. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your device

The app saves some information locally, using Apple's standard on-device storage. None of it leaves your phone or is visible to us:

  • Your current and longest daily streak
  • Quotes you've saved to your Library
  • Your reflections: the mood you picked and any thoughts you wrote about a quote
  • Your Bookshelf: the books you've added, your star ratings and your thoughts on them
  • How far you've got through the Bookclub tour
  • Whether you've turned on the daily or evening reminder, and what time you set
  • Your chosen accent colour theme

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Bookclub (optional)

Bookclub lets you join reading groups and chat. It only works with an account, so if you use it we store the following on our database provider, Supabase:

  • Your account: a sign-in identifier from Sign in with Apple. We don't ask Apple for your email address. If Apple shares your name the first time you sign in, we save it as your display name.
  • Your profile: your username, the book you're currently reading (if you add one), and when you agreed to the community rules.
  • Clubs: clubs you create or join, and your role in them.
  • What you post: messages, quoted passages, books you attach, and whether a message is marked as a spoiler.
  • Safety: reports you make, people you block, and, if an account breaks the rules, a record that it has been banned.

Other members can see your username, display name, currently-reading book and what you post in the clubs you share. Messages in public clubs can be read by anyone signed in to Bookclub. Reports and blocks are private.

We use this information only to run Bookclub and keep it safe, for example to review reported messages. We don't use it for advertising and don't share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Deleting your Bookclub account

You can delete your account at any time in Bookclub > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, messages, club memberships, reports and blocks. Clubs you started stay open for their other members. Your quotes, streak and reflections on your phone aren't affected. You can also delete individual messages by long-pressing them.

Book search and covers

When you search for a book (in Bookclub or for your Bookshelf), your search text is sent to Open Library (run by the Internet Archive) to find matching books, and book covers and descriptions are loaded from Open Library. When you scan a barcode, only the book's ISBN number is sent to Open Library to look it up. Their terms and privacy policy apply to those requests. To check that a book's Bookshop.org link works, the app also asks Bookshop.org whether it has a page for that ISBN; nothing about you is included.

Camera

If you choose to scan a book's barcode, Quotidian asks for permission to use your camera. The camera image is read on your phone to find the barcode; no photos or video are saved, and nothing from the camera is sent anywhere except the ISBN number described above. You can turn camera access off at any time in Settings > Quotidian.

Subscriptions & purchases

Quotidian offers an optional Premium subscription. All payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Quotidian never sees or stores your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active, so Premium features can be unlocked. You can view, manage, or cancel your subscription anytime in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Notifications

The optional daily and evening reminders are local notifications scheduled entirely on your device (the evening one changes its wording if you've already read the day's quote). Nothing about your usage of the app is sent anywhere to make this work.

Sharing quotes

When you share a quote, the text or image you choose is passed to the app you pick (Messages, for example). Shared quotes include a short line saying they came from Quotidian, with a link to the App Store.

Links to Bookshop.org

Tapping a book's purchase link opens its page on Bookshop.org in your browser. Quotidian earns a small affiliate commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. Once you leave Quotidian, your activity is covered by Bookshop.org's own privacy policy, and we can't see what you do there.

Service providers

  • Supabase: hosts the Bookclub database and sign-in.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, the App Store and subscriptions.
  • Open Library: book search and covers.

Your rights

You can see and change your Bookclub profile in the app, and delete your account and everything linked to it at any time. If you'd like a copy of your Bookclub data, or have any other request, email us at the address below. If you're in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children's privacy

Bookclub is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. The rest of the app collects no personal data from anyone.
