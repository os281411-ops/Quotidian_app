How streaks work

Your streak counts consecutive calendar days you've opened the app. Miss a day and it resets to 1. Milestones at 7, 30, 100, and 365 days get a special celebration.

Daily reminders

Turn on the daily reminder from the Profile tab to get a nudge at a time you choose. You can change or turn off the reminder time anytime in the same place.

Saving and reflecting

Tap the bookmark icon on any quote to add it to your Library. Tap Reflect to note how a quote made you feel, and add a thought if you like. Saved quotes and reflections stay on your device and aren't tied to an account.

Buying a book

Quotes from books link to that book on Bookshop.org, which supports independent bookstores with every purchase.

Bookclub

Bookclub lets you read along with others. Sign in with Apple in the Bookclub tab, pick a username and agree to the house rules, then join a public club or tap + to start one or join with an invite code. In a club you can chat, share passages, attach books and mark spoilers. The "Welcome to Bookclub" chat in My Clubs gives you a short tour.

Discuss today's quote: tap the gold speech-bubble button in Bookclub, or "Discuss today's quote" on the Today screen, to join that day's shared conversation.

Reporting and blocking: long-press any message to report it, or open someone's profile to report or block them. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You can unblock people in Bookclub > Account > Blocked Readers.

Deleting your account: Bookclub > Account > Delete Account permanently removes your profile and messages.

Bookshelf

In the Bookshelf tab, tap + to scan the barcode on the back of a book (this needs camera access) or search for it. Tap a cover to flip it and see the title, author and year; tap it again to open its page, where you can rate it out of five stars, write your thoughts and find it on Bookshop.org. Press and hold a cover to remove it. Your shelf is saved on your phone only.

Quotidian Premium

Premium lets you start your own book clubs and join private clubs with an invite, and unlocks unlimited saves, a home screen widget, shareable quote cards, a monthly streak freeze, accent colour themes, and a second daily reminder. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions — it's never handled inside the app itself.

Restoring a purchase

If Premium isn't showing up after reinstalling the app or switching devices, tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Short version: the daily quote, Library, Bookshelf, streaks and reflections stay on your phone; Bookclub stores your account and what you post so your clubs can see it.
